# ✨ LumeScribes

**An independent Italian game & mod localization guild**  
*Forging words. Connecting worlds.*

> **Every world deserves a voice.**

## 🌍 About the guild

LumeScribes is the public identity of **ITALIA LOCALIZATION TEAM (ILT)**, an independent initiative dedicated to bringing video games and mods from **English into Italian**.

We started with a simple idea: an indie spirit and the care of a professional workflow can belong together. We aim to preserve each game's tone, terminology and creative intent—not just translate its words.

## 🛠️ What we do

- **EN → IT localization** of interfaces, dialogue and mod content.
- **Linguistic review** for clarity, consistency and context.
- **Technical checks** of localization files, keys and placeholders.
- **Localization QA** and in-game testing when an appropriate test environment is available.
- **Documentation and maintenance** to support future updates.

## 🧭 Our approach

**Discover → Verify rights & sources → Translate → Review → Validate → Test → Release with approval**

We respect creators' licenses and contribution channels, credit original authors and contributors, and do not redistribute third-party assets without permission. A valid localization file is not, by itself, proof of a successful in-game test.

## 📜 Projects

Our first pilot localization is **in progress**. Project links, credits and downloads will appear here only when the required permissions, checks and release approvals are complete.
